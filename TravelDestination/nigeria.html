Nigeria

Explore Africa’s giant - full of natural wonders.

Overview

Nigeria is a vibrant country full of life, diversity, and culture. From the bustling city of Lagos to the sacred sites of Osun and the natural beauty of the Obudu Plateau, Nigeria is a colorful blend of traditions, landscapes, and modern charm.

Top Attractions

  • Olumo Rock – climb to panoramic views
  • Yankari National Park – wildlife and warm springs
  • Lekki Conservation Centre – canopy walk adventures
  • Erin Ijesha Waterfalls – seven cascading levels

Photo Gallery

Travel Tips

  • Best time to visit: November to April (dry season)
  • Embrace local markets and cuisine, try jollof rice!
  • Cash is king in many areas. Carry naira for local purchases
