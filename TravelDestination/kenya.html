Kenya

Experience the wild heart of Africa. From safaris to stunning highlands.

Overview

Kenya is a dream come true for nature lovers and adventure seekers. From the vast savannahs of the Maasai Mara to the coastal charm of Mombasa, Kenya offers unmatched diversity in culture, landscapes, and wildlife.

Top Attractions

  • Maasai Mara National Reserve – witness the Great Migration
  • Mount Kenya – second highest peak in Africa
  • Lake Nakuru – flamingo haven and rhino sanctuary
  • Diani Beach – white sands and turquoise waters

Photo Gallery

Travel Tips

  • Dry season (June to October) is best for safaris
  • Pack binoculars for wildlife viewing
  • Always carry mosquito repellent and sunscreen
