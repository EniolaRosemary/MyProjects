Travel Tips

  • Pack light, but don't forget essentials like adapters and travel documents.
  • Always have digital and physical copies of your ID and reservations.
  • Learn a few local phrases—it goes a long way!
  • Respect local customs and dress codes, especially in temples or sacred places.
  • Buy travel insurance for peace of mind.
