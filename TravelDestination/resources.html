Packing Tips

  • Pack light and layer-friendly clothing
  • Always carry a universal travel adapter
  • Include sunscreen, insect repellent, and a reusable water bottle
  • Keep digital and paper copies of your travel documents

Budgeting Advice

  • Book flights and accommodations in advance
  • Use apps to track expenses and convert currencies
  • Opt for local food and experiences to save more
  • Check for tourist cards or local transport passes
