Tanzania

Island paradise with rich history and pristine beaches.

Overview

Zanzibar is known for its white sandy beaches, turquoise waters, and a unique blend of African and Arabic culture. Located off the coast of Tanzania, it's a destination for relaxation, culture, and adventure.

Top Attractions

  • Stone Town – a UNESCO World Heritage site
  • Prison Island – home to giant tortoises
  • Kendwa and Nungwi beaches
  • Jozani Forest – red colobus monkeys and lush nature

Photo Gallery

Travel Tips

  • Best time to visit: June to October (dry season)
  • Respect local customs and dress modestly in towns
  • Use reef-safe sunscreen to protect marine life
